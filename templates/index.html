Key System Pillars

🌱

For Survivors

A gentle, non-intrusive daily check-in space. Track your stress, sleep, and emotional well-being without re-telling traumatic details. Receive supportive, non-diagnostic guidance tailored to your daily trends.

🤝

For Support Workers

Authorized counselors and NGO caseworkers access anonymized distress dashboards. Early-warning indicators highlight individuals who may benefit from gentle follow-up or care coordination.

🛡️

Privacy & Anonymity

Built on strict privacy-first principles. Each survivor is assigned a pseudonymous Anonymous ID (e.g. CB-7842). Personal contact information is kept strictly isolated from public views.

How CareBridge Works

Step 1

Daily Check-In

Answer a brief 5-question survey measuring stress, sleep, concentration, and support.

Step 2

ML Analysis

Interpretable AI model evaluates patterns to generate supportive distress risk indicators (Low, Moderate, High).

Step 3

Early Warning Alert

Rules detect sudden stress spikes or persistent poor sleep to suggest proactive follow-up.

Step 4

Care Coordination

Authorized support workers review anonymized indicators and offer timely assistance.

Need Immediate Assistance?

CareBridge is an automated monitoring system and cannot respond to immediate crisis events in real time. If you feel unsafe or require immediate medical assistance, please access local emergency services.
